# Instagram adapter as a whole

## Summary

The Instagram adapter fills the Platform-neutral contracts where it counts. IMessenger has one method per action and documents its lossy mappings. IMediaLibrary and IInboxConnector each have a second real adapter. The Universal Webhook lanes are pure translation, and Comments are a real Instagram-only capability extension: routes are gated by `requires_capability`, the hooks are optional, and the dispatcher has no Platform branch. The problems sit at the seams around the adapter. (1) The neutral contract and the domain layer import `MessageResult`, `ListSection` and `InteractiveHeader` from the WhatsApp package, so every Platform depends on WhatsApp. (2) The webhook-processor ABC is shaped like WhatsApp: Instagram stubs or implements members that only WhatsApp calls, because the only live seam is `create_universal_webhook`. (3) A whole parallel schema registry and `PlatformDetector` are dead code, yet `ARCHITECTURE.md` lists them as steps for adding a Platform. (4) `mark_as_read` declares its target optional, but Instagram requires it, and the shipped CLI examples call it without one. (5) Meta Graph calls and the Graph version default are copied by hand across the Instagram client, the subscriptions helper, the login core, the factory and settings. Smaller confirmed items: the domain `PrivateReply` validator names Instagram, the deauthorize route serves a login type the adapter does not support, `IMediaHandler` is a one-adapter seam in `domain/interfaces`, and the route capability struct that mounts routes for every Platform carries a WhatsApp name. The verifier rejected the auditor's proposal to move private replies into `ICommentControls`, because ADR-0014/0021 record the opposite and the move would lose middleware coverage. It also rejected the file split of `conversation_capabilities.py` (cosmetic) and the derived `supported_operations` (it adds machinery and is guarded by tests). ProfileReading is covered by audit 04.

## Confirmed findings

### 06-1 Neutral IMessenger contract, domain results and the Instagram adapter depend on WhatsApp-package types

- Files: `wappa/messaging/instagram/messenger.py`, `wappa/domain/interfaces/messaging_interface.py`, `wappa/domain/results.py`, `wappa/core/messaging/pipeline.py`, `wappa/messaging/whatsapp/models/basic_models.py`, `wappa/messaging/whatsapp/models/interactive_models.py`
- Principle: platform-leak
- Severity: high
- Breaking: yes. Public surfaces: the `wappa.messaging` exports keep their names, but deep imports from `wappa.messaging.whatsapp.models.basic_models` / `interactive_models` for these types break. Also update the `docs/public-contract.md` sentence on interactive values and result housing (around line 56) and `wappa/messaging/ARCHITECTURE.md:64-66`.
- Evidence: `wappa/messaging/instagram/messenger.py:60-64` imports `MessageResult` from `wappa.messaging.whatsapp.models.basic_models` and `InteractiveHeader`, `ListSection` from `wappa.messaging.whatsapp.models.interactive_models`. The neutral contract does the same under `TYPE_CHECKING` (`wappa/domain/interfaces/messaging_interface.py:21-26`), as does `wappa/core/messaging/pipeline.py:52-56`. The domain layer imports the adapter package at runtime: `wappa/domain/results.py:22` `from wappa.messaging.whatsapp.models.basic_models import MessageResult`. ADR-0014 (`docs/adr/0014-instagram-text-and-explicit-messenger-targets.md`, "Trade-off and consequences") records only `MessageResult` housing as a follow-up. `ListSection` and `InteractiveHeader` are not recorded, although `ReplyButton` and `MessageCard` already live neutrally in `wappa/domain/interactive.py`.
- Deletion test: this is not a module deletion. The dependency arrow points the wrong way: a third Platform adapter must import `wappa.messaging.whatsapp.*` to satisfy IMessenger, and `wappa.domain` cannot be imported without the WhatsApp adapter package.
- Proposed fix: move `MessageResult` into `wappa/domain/results.py`, and move `ListSection`, `ListRow`, `InteractiveHeader` and `HeaderType` into `wappa/domain/interactive.py`. Point IMessenger, the pipeline and both adapters at the new homes. Delete the old WhatsApp-module homes outright (clean cut, no re-export) and list the deep-import break in CHANGELOG. WhatsApp-specific payload builders stay in the WhatsApp package.
- ADR conflict: none. This completes the follow-up that ADR-0014 recorded.

### 06-2 Webhook-processor seam is a WhatsApp-shaped fat ABC; Instagram implements members nobody calls

- Files: `wappa/processors/base_processor.py`, `wappa/processors/instagram_processor.py`, `wappa/webhooks/instagram/models.py`, `wappa/webhooks/core/base_webhook.py`, `wappa/core/inbound/runtime.py`, `wappa/core/wappa_app.py`, `wappa/processors/factory.py`
- Principle: missing-interface
- Severity: medium
- Breaking: no. Public surfaces: `BaseWebhookProcessor`, `ProcessorCapabilities` and `BaseWebhook` are internal (none of them appears in `docs/public-contract.md` or `wappa/__init__.py`).
- Evidence: `BaseWebhookProcessor` declares seven abstract members (`base_processor.py:90-170`): `platform`, `capabilities`, `create_universal_webhook`, `parse_webhook_container`, `get_supported_message_types`, `create_message_from_data` and `create_status_from_data`. The runtime calls only `processor.create_universal_webhook` (`wappa/core/inbound/runtime.py:310`). Instagram satisfies the rest with dead or stub code. `create_status_from_data` raises `NotImplementedError` (`instagram_processor.py:165-170`). `create_message_from_data` accepts only TEXT and raises otherwise (`:157-163`). `parse_webhook_container` is called only by Instagram's own `create_universal_webhook` (`:185`). `capabilities` builds a `ProcessorCapabilities` (`:144-149`) whose only readers are `ProcessorFactory.get_processor_capabilities` (`processors/factory.py:350`) and `get_processing_stats` (`base_processor.py:198`), and neither of those has a caller outside `processors/`. `InstagramWebhook` also implements the WhatsApp-shaped container ABC: `get_raw_messages`, `get_raw_statuses`, `get_contacts`, `has_errors`, `get_metadata` and `InstagramMetadata` (`webhooks/instagram/models.py:300-393`). Those methods are invoked only by WhatsApp's processor on WhatsApp containers (`whatsapp_processor.py:739, 867, 1347, 1406`) and by the base class's own `get_processing_context` (`base_webhook.py:252-254`). The one real extra hook, `set_field_registry`, is missing from the base class, so `wappa/core/wappa_app.py:287-294` isinstance-checks WhatsApp but probes Instagram with `getattr`. The auditor's claim that `is_user_message` and `is_text_message` are consumed by WhatsApp internals is wrong: their callers are inside `webhooks/instagram/models.py` (`:348, :362, :420`). The conclusion still holds.
- Deletion test: deleting Instagram's stub members, `InstagramMetadata` and its container-ABC overrides removes roughly 100+ lines without changing any caller. The ABC imposes this complexity; Instagram does not need it.
- Proposed fix: shrink the shared seam to what the runtime uses: `platform`, `async create_universal_webhook(payload) -> UniversalWebhook` and `set_field_registry(registry) -> None` with a no-op default. Move the remaining members into `WhatsAppWebhookProcessor` as private methods, keep `InstagramWebhook` as a plain pydantic envelope, and replace the `getattr` probe at `wappa_app.py:292` with a loop over registered processors calling the interface. Delete `ProcessorCapabilities` and `get_processing_stats` if nothing else reads them after 06-3 (do not add a second consumer to justify them).
- ADR conflict: none. ADR-0010 and the root `ARCHITECTURE.md` "Adding a new platform" describe the processor in prose but do not mandate these members.

### 06-4 mark_as_read: the target Instagram requires is optional in the neutral signature, and shipped examples omit it

- Files: `wappa/domain/interfaces/messaging_interface.py`, `wappa/messaging/instagram/messenger.py`, `wappa/messaging/whatsapp/messenger/whatsapp_messenger.py`, `wappa/core/messaging/pipeline.py`, `wappa/api/routes/whatsapp/whatsapp_messages.py`, `wappa/cli/examples/`
- Principle: LSP
- Severity: medium
- Breaking: yes. Public surfaces: the `IMessenger.mark_as_read` signature (target becomes required and positional order changes to `message_id, target, typing=False`), the mark-as-read HTTP request model (`recipient` becomes required), the `docs/public-contract.md` Messenger operations table, the wappa-instagram-apps skill `references/text-and-media.md`, and the CLI example apps.
- Evidence: `IMessenger.mark_as_read(message_id, typing=False, target: DirectMessage | None = None)` (`messaging_interface.py:89-94`). The rule is carried in the docstring prose instead: "Instagram ... needs ``target``. Passing ``target`` everywhere keeps host code Platform-neutral" (`:95-102`). Instagram raises `ValueError` when target is None (`instagram/messenger.py:734-738`). WhatsApp only checks the target when one is given (`whatsapp_messenger.py:290-296`). The HTTP route passes `None` when `recipient` is absent (`whatsapp_messages.py:163-175`), so on an Instagram Inbox it fails at runtime. The shipped scaffold examples call it without a target, for example `wappa/cli/examples/simple_echo_example/app/master_event.py:64` and `wappa/cli/examples/openai_transcript/app/master_event.py:24`, so a Host that copies them gets code that fails on Instagram. ADR-0016 set out to remove exactly this kind of Platform-specific branching.
- Deletion test: making target required deletes Instagram's runtime `ValueError` and the prose caveat in the interface, the public contract and the skill. WhatsApp loses nothing, because it already accepts and checks the target.
- Proposed fix: make target required on `IMessenger.mark_as_read` and on both adapters and the pipeline passthrough (`pipeline.py:295-303`). Make `recipient` required on the HTTP request model. Update the CLI examples to pass `DirectMessage` targets (they already receive the webhook's user). List the break in CHANGELOG.
- ADR conflict: none. ADR-0016 rule 1 describes the call with a target.

### 06-5 Meta Graph request and outcome classification are copied per call site; the Graph version default is repeated five times

- Files: `wappa/messaging/instagram/graph.py`, `wappa/connections/instagram.py`, `wappa/connections/meta_login.py`, `wappa/domain/factories/messenger_factory.py`, `wappa/core/config/meta_application.py`, `wappa/core/config/settings.py`
- Principle: DRY
- Severity: medium
- Breaking: no, unless Hosts construct `InstagramSubscriptions` or the login config relying on its defaults. In that case the `graph_api_version` / `graph_base_url` defaults move to `MetaApplicationConfig`.
- Evidence: `InstagramGraphClient.request` (`messaging/instagram/graph.py:112-169`) is the canonical bearer call, with transport failure mapped to INDETERMINATE, 5xx or malformed 2xx to INDETERMINATE, and 4xx to REJECTED. `InstagramSubscriptions` rebuilds the bearer header and the same tri-state mapping by hand in `_subscribe_call` (`connections/instagram.py:127-147`), again with different semantics in `subscribed_fields` (`:181-196`), and again in `unsubscribe` (`:208-221`). The client cannot be reused there because its constructor binds an `inbox_ref` and token (`graph.py:91-103`). The literal `"v26.0"` appears in `core/config/meta_application.py:38`, `core/config/settings.py:90`, `connections/meta_login.py:51`, `connections/instagram.py:118` and `domain/factories/messenger_factory.py:138`. The literal `https://graph.facebook.com` appears at `meta_application.py:39`, `settings.py:91`, `meta_login.py:52`, `connections/instagram.py:119` and `messenger_factory.py:139`. A Graph version bump that misses one site silently splits Inbox traffic across API versions.
- Deletion test: a token-per-call Graph request function would delete the three hand-rolled Instagram subscription copies, and taking the version and base URL only from `MetaApplicationConfig` deletes four of the five literals. Today a change to outcome semantics, such as treating 429 differently, has to be made in several places.
- Proposed fix: keep it small. Extract the body of `InstagramGraphClient.request` into a module-level `graph_request(client, base, path, token, *, operation, ...) -> GraphResponse` in `messaging/instagram/graph.py` (or a Meta-neutral `wappa/connections/meta_graph.py` if `MetaBusinessLogin` adopts it too). Then let `InstagramGraphClient` and `InstagramSubscriptions` call it. Remove the literal defaults from `meta_login.py`, `connections/instagram.py` and `messenger_factory.py`, and require the values from `MetaApplicationConfig`. Do not build a `MetaGraphTransport` class hierarchy, and leave `WhatsAppClient` out of scope: its result shape differs and it belongs to audit 02/07.
- ADR conflict: none.

- Files: `wappa/api/routes/webhooks.py`, `wappa/connections/instagram.py`, `wappa/connections/__init__.py`, `docs/public-contract.md`, `docs/adr/0016-one-messenger-method-per-action.md`

## Rejected findings

- 06-2 (auditor) "PrivateReply welded into the shared MessageTarget union; move it to ICommentControls.send_private_reply": rejected. ADR-0014 deferred `PrivateReply` to the comment phase as a target. ADR-0021 rule 6 records that "the private reply belongs to outbound transport", and the `ICommentControls` docstring (`conversation_capabilities.py:115`) follows it. A private reply is a send: it needs the pipeline's middleware (window guards see `SendInvocation.target.address`, `pipeline.py:93-99`, which is generic, not a special case) and the `ISentMessageRegistry` echo record in `_send` (`instagram/messenger.py:298-325`). Moving it would either lose that coverage or duplicate the plumbing in the capability. A comment-addressed target is a real address kind, not an Instagram concept, because Facebook Page messaging has the same mechanism. The legitimate residue, the Platform name in the domain validator, is kept as confirmed 06-6.
- 06-9 (auditor, first half) "Split conversation_capabilities.py into neutral read primitives and an Instagram-extension module": rejected as cosmetic. ADR-0016 rule 4 makes capability interfaces neutral contracts that a Platform may or may not implement. `ICommentControls` and `IConversationContentReader` are named for the action, not the Platform, and they belong in `domain/interfaces`. Splitting the file changes no dependency. Moving `IAttachmentMediaReader` under `wappa/messaging/instagram` would put a capability contract inside an adapter, which is the wrong direction. The WhatsApp-named route struct half is kept as confirmed 06-9.
- 06-10 (auditor) "supported_operations is a hand-maintained string set": rejected. The two literal sets (`instagram/messenger.py:175-190`, `whatsapp_messenger.py:91-110`) are short, explicit and covered by tests (`tests/test_instagram_conversations.py:878-885`). Deriving them by reflection over overridden methods would add machinery that cannot express `send_text:private_reply`, which is a real capability distinction for Hosts and not a pseudo-operation. The auditor itself deferred the fix. That is over-engineering for no present variation.
- 06-8 (auditor, IAttachmentMediaReader and per-call library construction parts): rejected. `IAttachmentMediaReader` is a legitimate Instagram capability (size-limited, streaming reads) that `InstagramMediaLibrary` delegates to. WhatsApp building a new `WhatsAppMediaLibrary` per `capability()` call (`whatsapp_messenger.py:86-87`) is a cheap, stateless wrapper and not a defect. The `IMediaHandler` part is kept as confirmed 06-8.

## Checked and legitimate

- `wappa/messaging/instagram/messenger.py` lossy mappings (captions as follow-up text, lists to quick replies, location and contact as text, carousel intro text): ADR-0016 rule 3 (presentation dropped, meaning never), documented in docstrings and `wappa/messaging/ARCHITECTURE.md:230`.
- `capability(kind)` / `require_capability(kind)` on `IMessenger`: the right discovery seam. Comment routes in `wappa/api/routes/conversations.py` gate on `requires_capability(ICommentControls)` with no Platform branch.
- `wappa/domain/factories/messenger_factory.py:130-143` and `ProcessorFactory._create_processor`: the Platform `if/elif` constructs the adapter inside the composition root, which is the right home. Only the literal defaults inside it are a finding (06-5).
- `wappa/api/controllers/webhook_controller.py` allowed-Platform set and the choice of payload router: the callback composition root, one branch per Platform, where the payload shapes really differ.
- `wappa/core/inbound/instagram_routing.py`: signature-first, whole-batch Inbox admission that mirrors WhatsApp routing on the shared `RoutedWebhookDelivery`. Two routers with different envelope semantics do not justify a shared abstraction.
- `wappa/processors/instagram_processor.py` `create_universal_webhook` onward: pure translation into the Universal Webhook lanes with typed refs and schema-drift reporting. The issue is only the ABC around it (06-2).
- `InstagramMediaLibrary` and `InstagramConnector`: second real adapters of `IMediaLibrary` and `IInboxConnector` (ADR-0017), so both are real seams.
- `IncomingCommentWebhook` and mention events dispatched to optional log-only hooks: acceptable under ADR-0016 rule 4. A Platform without them never emits them.
- The `send_text:private_reply` entry in `supported_operations`: a real support distinction Hosts need, not a stringly hack (see Rejected 06-10).
- wappa-instagram-apps skill: API names, limits and the Unified Login deauthorize caveat match the code. The only wording nit is that "history sync" in the exclusions list should read "history push sync", because `IConversationHistoryReader` (pull) is supported.

## Interface proposal

The adapter needs no new abstraction. It needs the shared seams trimmed to what is really called, plus the WhatsApp types moved out of the neutral contract.

1. PlatformWebhooks: `IPlatformWebhookProcessor { platform: PlatformType; async create_universal_webhook(payload: dict) -> UniversalWebhook; set_field_registry(registry: FieldHandlerRegistry | None) -> None  # no-op default }`. `InstagramWebhookProcessor` already satisfies it once the WhatsApp-shaped members are dropped (06-2), and `ProcessorFactory` stays the only Platform registry (06-3).
2. SendingMessages: `IMessenger` keeps its shape (ADR-0016, one method per action). `MessageResult`, `ListSection`, `ListRow`, `InteractiveHeader` and `HeaderType` move to `wappa.domain` (06-1). `mark_as_read(message_id, target, typing=False)` takes a required target (06-4). `MessageTarget = DirectMessage | PrivateReply` stays (ADR-0014/0021) without a Platform name in the domain validator (06-6).
3. HandlingMedia: `IMediaLibrary { upload(...); download(media_id) -> ReadResult[MediaBytes] }` is the one neutral seam, and it already has two adapters. `IMediaHandler` becomes WhatsApp-internal (06-8). `IAttachmentMediaReader` stays an Instagram capability.
4. ProfileReading: defer to audit 04. On Instagram it would be one capability method over `GET /{IGSID}` through `InstagramGraphClient`, registered in `InstagramMessenger.capability()` beside `IConversationHistoryReader`.
5. InboxConnection: `IInboxConnector` (ADR-0017) with two adapters on the shared `MetaBusinessLogin` core. Drop the unreachable Instagram deauthorize route (06-7). `InstagramSubscriptions` shares the Graph request function with `InstagramGraphClient`, and the version and base URL come only from `MetaApplicationConfig` (06-5).

Instagram-only extensions, unchanged in shape: `ICommentControls` (reply, hide, unhide, delete, comments-enabled) and `IConversationContentReader` (post, comment, lists, mention reads), both discovered through `capability()` and gated per route with `requires_capability`. Private replies stay a `send_text` target.
